## Purpose
A design-lead workflow for building distinctive interfaces based on the product, audience, and brief rather than generic templates. It guides typography, color, layout, content, responsive behavior, accessibility, and restrained motion.

## Workflow
1. Ground the design in the product and audience.
2. Plan color tokens, typography, layout, and design principles.
3. Critique the plan for generic or overused AI design patterns.
4. Implement only after the design direction is coherent.
5. Self-review responsive behavior, keyboard focus, accessibility, and reduced-motion support.

## Best use
Use for new pages, redesigns, landing pages, dashboards, and visual direction before implementation. Pair with Unslop when auditing an existing interface.

## Compatibility
Primarily design guidance; can inform React, Vue, and PHP-rendered HTML/CSS. It does not require a specific frontend runtime by itself.
