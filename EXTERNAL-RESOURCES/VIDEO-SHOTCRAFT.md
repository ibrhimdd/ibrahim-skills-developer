## Purpose
Creates cinematic product videos from frontend projects or webpages using real page captures, shot recipe cards, storyboards, Remotion demos/templates, music beat-sync, sound design, and final quality review.

The source describes 124 shot recipe cards, demo implementations, a motion-preview gallery, a complete video template, reusable components, and sound/music workflows.

## Best use
- Promotional videos for websites and applications.
- Feature walkthroughs and product launches.
- Cinematic shots for product interfaces.
- Sound-effect selection, timing, and music synchronization.

## Workflow
The full production pipeline covers product understanding, visual direction, feature-to-shot mapping, storyboarding, final asset capture, shot implementation, sound design, and independent final review. The source documents direct-template, autonomous free-creation, and guided-creation modes.

## Requirements and limitations
The production workflow is Remotion-based. Check the current README and project setup for Node, browser, FFmpeg, and rendering prerequisites. Optional JianYing export tooling is marked Mac-tested / Windows-untested in the repository; do not assume that optional path works on Windows.
